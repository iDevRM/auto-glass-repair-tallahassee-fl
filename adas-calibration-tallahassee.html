What Does ADAS Camera Calibration Involve?

Calibration starts with a scan of the vehicle’s computer. For a static calibration, the car is parked on level ground and a printed target is set up at an exact distance and height in front of it, then the scan tool tells the camera to find the target. For a dynamic calibration, the technician drives the car at set speeds on roads with clear lane lines, such as Capital Circle, while the camera relearns them. Some vehicles need one method, some need both.

When Do You Need ADAS Camera Calibration?

After a new windshield

The camera looks at the road through the glass, so new glass changes its aim.

A lane-keep warning light

A dash alert after glass work points to a camera that needs calibration.

Odd steering or braking

Assist systems that tug or brake at the wrong time can be misaimed.

After a front-end repair

Suspension or alignment work can change the camera’s view.

A cracked camera area

Damage near the mirror mount affects what the camera sees.

A new mirror bracket

Moving the camera mount means it has to be aimed again.

Why Does a New Windshield Throw Off the Camera?

The forward camera is mounted to the windshield and looks out through it. A tiny change in the glass angle, thickness or mounting spot shifts where the camera thinks the road is. One degree off can put its view of a lane several feet to the side by the time it is looking far down the road. That is enough to make warnings and automatic braking unreliable. Drivers usually notice in the first week, when they call and ask, “Why is my lane-keep light on?”

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my car needs ADAS calibration?

If your vehicle has lane-keep assist, lane departure warning, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise, and the camera sits behind the rearview mirror, it almost always needs calibration after a new windshield. We check by VIN when we quote.

How long does ADAS calibration take?

Most calibrations take 30 minutes to an hour after the windshield is installed. Dynamic calibrations also depend on traffic and road conditions.

What happens if I skip calibration?

The safety systems may work poorly or not at all. Warnings can come late, the car may brake for no reason, or lane-keep can steer you toward the line instead of away from it. Having the car brake hard for nothing on Thomasville Road is unnerving.

Is calibration covered by insurance?

When the windshield is replaced under a covered claim and the vehicle requires calibration, most insurers include it. We confirm with your insurer before the work.
