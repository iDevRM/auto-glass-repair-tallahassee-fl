What Does Car Window Replacement Involve?

The inside door panel or trim comes off first. The technician vacuums the shattered glass out of the door, the window tracks and the cabin, then fits the new glass to the window regulator and adjusts it so it rolls up evenly and seals against the weatherstrip. For a back window, the new glass is bonded in place and the defrost and antenna connections are hooked back up and tested.

When Do You Need Car Window Replacement?

A break-in

Thieves smash a side window and leave glass all over the seats and floor.

Glass that dropped into the door

A failed regulator or clip can crack the glass or leave it stuck.

A cracked back window

Rear glass can shatter from a hit, a slammed hatch or a sudden temperature change.

Broken quarter window

Small fixed windows behind the doors are easy to miss and easy to break.

Tree or hail damage

Falling limbs and summer hail break side and back glass.

A window that will not seal

Wind noise or leaks after an old repair can mean the glass sits wrong.

Why Does Side Window Glass Shatter Into Small Pieces?

Most side and back windows are tempered glass. It is heated and cooled fast at the factory, which makes it much stronger than ordinary glass. When it does break, it bursts into small rounded pieces instead of sharp shards, which is safer for anyone in the car. The catch is that it cannot be repaired. Any crack at all means a new window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you replace a car window the same day?

Often, yes. Common door glass is usually easy to get, and the install takes about an hour. Less common back glass may need to be ordered, and we tell you the timing when we quote.

Do you clean up the broken glass?

Yes. The technician vacuums glass out of the door, the window tracks, the seats and the floor. Tiny pieces can hide deep in a door, so we also run the window up and down to clear them.

Will insurance cover a broken car window in Florida?

Break-ins and storm damage are usually covered if you carry comprehensive coverage. Unlike a windshield, side and back glass are subject to your deductible, so check that number before you file.

What should I do until the window is replaced?

Park in a garage if you can, take valuables out and cover the opening with heavy plastic taped to the frame, not the paint. Then call us and we will book the earliest open time. The question we hear most is “Can you get here before it rains?” Usually, yes.
