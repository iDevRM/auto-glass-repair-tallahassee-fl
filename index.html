The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in Tallahassee?

Auto glass repair in Tallahassee covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or SUV. That means filling rock chips with resin, putting in a new windshield, replacing a side or back window, and re-aiming the safety camera that looks out through the windshield. Drivers on I-10, US-27 and Capital Circle pick up a lot of chips from trucks and road work.

A windshield is also a safety part. It is glued to the body with urethane, it helps hold the roof up in a rollover and the passenger airbag pushes against it, so the install matters as much as the glass.

Cost

How Much Does Windshield Replacement Cost in Tallahassee?

For most insured drivers, nothing. Florida Statute 627.7288 bars insurers from applying your deductible to windshield damage when you carry comprehensive coverage. People are often surprised by that, and plenty of drivers ride around with a cracked windshield for months because they assume it will cost them.

Paying out of pocket, the cost of windshield replacement in Tallahassee comes down to what is built into the glass. Rain sensors, heated wiper areas, acoustic layers and heads-up display glass need more specialized parts, and a forward camera adds a calibration step. Side and back glass are not covered by the zero-deductible law, so those go through your regular deductible. Call and we will check your vehicle and your coverage before any work starts.

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in Tallahassee

The question we hear most on the phone is “Will this cost me anything?” It is the first answer below.

Does insurance pay for a new windshield in Florida?

Usually, yes. If your policy has comprehensive coverage, Florida Statute 627.7288 says your deductible does not apply to windshield damage, so most drivers pay nothing out of pocket for a new windshield. Side windows and back glass are not part of that law and go through your regular deductible.

Can a windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Often it can. A chip smaller than a quarter, or one crack shorter than about six inches, can usually be filled with resin as long as it is not at the edge of the glass or right in front of the driver. Tell us the size and where it sits and we will say which fix fits.

Why do windshield chips spread so fast in Tallahassee?

Heat is the usual cause. Glass that has baked in a parking lot all afternoon gets hit with cold air from the AC, and the uneven temperature pulls at the edges of the chip. Bumps on rough roads and slammed doors add more stress, so a chip left alone through a North Florida summer rarely stays small.

Do you come to my home, office or campus?

Yes. Tallahassee Auto Glass Repair is a mobile service, so the technician works in your driveway, an apartment lot or the lot at your job. The address on this site is a service office, not a shop where you drop the car off.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The install takes about an hour. After that the urethane adhesive needs one to three hours before the car is safe to drive, depending on the product and the weather. A chip repair is much quicker, usually about 30 minutes.

What happens if it rains during my appointment?

The glass and the frame have to be dry for the adhesive to bond. Summer storms here usually roll in mid-afternoon, so we book morning slots when the forecast looks wet, or work under a carport, garage or parking deck.

Does my car need camera calibration after a new windshield?

If the vehicle has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise control, the camera behind the rearview mirror usually needs to be calibrated after the glass is replaced. We check for it when we give the quote.
