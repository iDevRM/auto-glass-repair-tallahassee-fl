What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

The job starts with covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond and trims the old adhesive down to a thin, even layer. Any bare metal on the frame gets primed so rust cannot start under the new seal. Then fresh urethane goes down, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, sensors and moldings go back on. Plan on about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between glass and frame and spread fast.

Several chips at once

Three or more chips, common for I-10 commuters, add up to weak glass.

A leak at the top corners

Water inside after a storm often points to a failed seal.

Pitting that glares at night

Years of sand and grit leave tiny pits that make headlights flare.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Spread in Florida Heat?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a rock chip breaks the outer layer. Glass expands as it heats and shrinks as it cools, and every cycle pulls at the ends of the break. In Tallahassee the worst moment is a car that sat in the sun at an FSU or state office lot all afternoon, then gets the AC turned to max. Rough patches on older roads add a jolt on top of that. A chip picked up in April is often a crack across the glass by the end of July.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, depending on the product and the weather. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Is OEM glass better than aftermarket glass?

OEM glass comes from the same supplier that made your original windshield. Good aftermarket glass meets the same federal safety rules, but on cars with cameras or heads-up displays OEM glass can make calibration easier. We tell you which options fit your vehicle and your insurance.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash, and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days so the new seal is not disturbed.

Will a windshield claim raise my insurance rates in Florida?

A glass-only claim under comprehensive coverage is generally treated as a no-fault claim. If you are worried about it, call your agent before filing, or ask us and we will walk you through what the insurer will ask.
