What Does Windshield Chip Repair Involve?

First the chip gets cleaned and any moisture inside is dried out. A bridge tool is mounted over the damage with suction cups. It pulls the air out of the break, then pushes clear resin in under pressure. A UV lamp cures the resin hard, and the surface is scraped and polished flat. Most chips end up much harder to see, and they stop spreading.

When Do You Need Windshield Chip Repair?

A bullseye or star chip

Round and star-shaped chips under a quarter are the best candidates.

Short crack off a chip

A single crack under about six inches can often still be filled.

A chip before summer

Filling it before the hot months keeps the heat from running it.

Leased car with a chip

A clean repair helps avoid a charge for damaged glass at turn-in.

Fresh hit from the interstate

Stones from trucks on I-10 and US-27 are the usual cause.

A chip outside the driver’s view

Repairs leave a faint mark, so they work best away from the driver’s eye line.

Why Does a Small Windshield Chip Turn Into a Crack?

A chip is a break in the outer layer of glass, and every change in temperature stresses its edges. Summer sun on a dark dashboard, cold AC on hot glass and the odd cool morning in January all make the glass flex. Potholes and slammed doors add shocks. Pollen, dirt and water that get into an open chip also make a clean repair harder later on. People ask, “Can it wait until next month?” Sometimes. In August, usually not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a windshield chip repair take?

Most chip repairs take about 30 minutes on site, and you can drive right away. The resin is cured with UV light, so there is no waiting for it to dry.

Will the chip disappear completely?

Usually it gets much harder to see, but a faint mark often stays. The main goal is to stop the chip from spreading and to give the glass back its strength.

Should I put tape over a chip until it is fixed?

Clear tape over the chip keeps dirt, pollen and water out until the repair. Also try not to aim the AC straight at the glass on a hot day, since that can make the chip run.

Can you repair a chip in the rain?

The chip has to be dry for the resin to flow in, so we work under a carport, garage or parking deck, or move the time to a dry part of the day.
